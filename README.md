# image-to-excel-sales-analysis
Converted image-based sales records into a structured spreadsheet using Google Sheets. Performed data cleaning, analysis with spreadsheet formulas, created charts, and produced business insights and recommendations to demonstrate practical data analysis and reporting skills.

📊 Project #6: Image-to-Excel Sales Analysis

Overview

This project demonstrates the process of converting unstructured sales data from an image into a structured spreadsheet for analysis. The data was manually extracted, cleaned, analyzed using spreadsheet formulas, and visualized with charts to generate business insights and recommendations.

Objective

The objective of this project was to practice data extraction, spreadsheet analysis, and business reporting by transforming image-based sales records into actionable insights.

Dataset

- Source: Sample sales record image
- Period: 1 July 2026 – 7 July 2026
- Records: 21 sales transactions
- Products: Rice 2kg, Sugar 1kg, and Cooking Oil 1L

Tools Used

- Google Sheets
- Google Docs
- Microsoft Excel (file compatibility)

Skills Demonstrated

- Data Entry
- Data Cleaning
- Spreadsheet Formula Development
- Data Aggregation
- Data Visualization
- Business Analysis
- Report Writing

Spreadsheet Functions Used

- "SUM"
- "AVERAGE"
- "MAX"
- "MIN"
- "COUNTA"
- "SUMIF"

Visualizations

The project includes:

- Revenue by Item (Column Chart)
- Daily Revenue Trend (Line Chart)

Key Findings

- A total of 21 sales transactions were analyzed.
- Cooking Oil 1L generated the highest total revenue during the analysis period.
- Daily sales revenue fluctuated across the week, indicating changing customer demand.
- Summary metrics and charts made it easier to evaluate product performance.

Business Recommendations

- Maintain sufficient inventory of Cooking Oil 1L due to its strong revenue contribution.
- Monitor daily sales trends to improve inventory planning.
- Introduce promotional strategies for lower-performing products.
- Continue maintaining structured digital sales records to support data-driven decision-making.

Project Files

- "SalesData.xlsx" – Spreadsheet containing the cleaned dataset, formulas, metrics, and charts.
- "SalesReport.pdf" – Final business report.
- "Revenue_by_Item.png" – Column chart.
- "Daily_Revenue_Trend.png" – Line chart.
